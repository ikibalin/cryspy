# Profile matching with fixed integrated intensities: the intensities of the loop pd_peak
# (from a profile matching, a Rietveld calculation or given by the user) are kept, the peak
# positions and shapes are refined.
Recipe refine_with_fixed_intensities: Refinement of the cell and the peak shape with the fixed integrated intensities of pd_peak
Requests: fixed intensities, keep the intensities of pd_peak, refine the cell with fixed intensities, refine the peak shape with fixed intensities, matching mode with given intensities
For: any

1. start: Fix all and calculate with the intensities of pd_peak. Without pd_peak (an error) calculate first, or use the recipe profile_matching.
   Actions: fix_parameters; profile_matching {"fixed_intensities": true}
   Success: chi_sq/n is a number.
   Otherwise: Stop and explain the error.
2. zero: Zero shift alone.
   Actions: refine_parameters {"pattern": "offset_ttheta"}; profile_matching {"fixed_intensities": true}
   Otherwise: undo_refinement
3. cell: Unit cell parameters.
   Actions: refine_parameters {"pattern": "unit cell"}; profile_matching {"fixed_intensities": true}
   Otherwise: undo_refinement
4. width: Peak width W, then U and V.
   Actions: refine_parameters {"pattern": "pd_instr_resolution.w"}; profile_matching {"fixed_intensities": true}; refine_parameters {"pattern": "pd_instr_resolution.u, pd_instr_resolution.v"}; profile_matching {"fixed_intensities": true}
   Otherwise: undo_refinement and keep W only.
5. background: Intensities of the background points.
   Actions: refine_parameters {"pattern": "pd_background"}; profile_matching {"fixed_intensities": true}
   Otherwise: undo_refinement

Final answer: chi_sq/n at the start and at the end, the refined parameters with their errors; the intensities of pd_peak are not changed.
