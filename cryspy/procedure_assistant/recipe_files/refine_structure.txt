# Dispatcher kept for the old requests: the recipe for each kind of unpolarized experiment.
Recipe refine_structure: Step-by-step refinement of a crystal structure
Requests: refine the structure, refine from scratch, fit my data, improve the fit of the model to the data
For: unpolarized

1. powder: 1D powder diffraction with constant wavelength.
   When: powder_1d
   Actions: use_recipe {"name": "refine_powder"}
2. tof: Time-of-flight powder diffraction.
   When: tof
   Actions: use_recipe {"name": "refine_tof"}
3. single_crystal: Single-crystal integrated intensities.
   When: single_crystal, unpolarized
   Actions: use_recipe {"name": "refine_single_crystal"}

Final answer: the recipes used, chi_sq/n (and R_F2 for single crystals) at the start and at the end, the steps done, skipped and undone with the reason, and what diagnose_fit still reports.
