# Flip ratios of a paramagnet in a field (polarized neutrons, diffrn_refln_fr), nuclear space
# group: the susceptibility tensors. Flip ratios do not depend on the scale; the nuclear
# structure and the extinction come from unpolarized data of the same crystal and stay fixed.
Recipe refine_flip_ratio_nuclear: Refinement of flip ratios with a nuclear space group
Requests: refine the flip ratios, refine the susceptibility, the magnetization of a paramagnet, polarized neutrons on a single crystal
For: flip_ratio, nuclear

1. start: Fix all parameters and check that the model gives magnetic scattering; check_flip_ratio_model asks the user for a missing field, polarization or magnetic atom.
   Actions: fix_parameters; check_flip_ratio_model
   Success: the calculated flip ratios are not all equal to 1.
   Otherwise: Stop and explain what is missing in the model.
2. chi_iso: Isotropic susceptibility (Ciso) of the magnetic atoms, from a start value when it is 0.
   Actions: set_chi_type {"chi_type": "Ciso"}; set_parameter {"path": "chi_11", "value": 0.1, "if_zero": true}; refine_parameters {"pattern": "chi_11"}; refine
3. chi_aniso: Anisotropic susceptibility (Cani): the components allowed by the site symmetry. Keep it only when chi_sq/n gets clearly better (more than 5 %).
   Actions: set_chi_type {"chi_type": "Cani"}; refine_parameters {"pattern": "atom_site_susceptibility"}; refine
4. kappa: Expansion of the magnetic form factor, only when chi_sq/n is still above 2 or diagnose_fit reports TREND; it correlates with the susceptibility.
   Actions: refine_parameters {"pattern": "kappa"}; refine
5. check: What is still wrong.
   Actions: use_recipe {"name": "check_result"}

Final answer: chi_sq/n of the start model and at the end, the susceptibility tensors of the magnetic atoms with sigmas, the steps done, skipped and undone with the reason. The beam polarization and the extinction are refined only when the user asks.
