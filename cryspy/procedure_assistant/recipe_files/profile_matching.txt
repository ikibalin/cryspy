# Profile matching (Le Bail / Pawley fit with intensities >= 0) of a 1D powder pattern: the
# integrated intensities are fitted at every step, so the cell, the zero shift, the peak shape
# and the background are refined without a structure model (FullProf Jbt=2).
Recipe profile_matching: Profile matching (Le Bail fit) of a powder pattern with free integrated intensities
Requests: profile matching, Le Bail fit, Pawley fit, matching mode, fit the intensities of the reflections, refine the cell without a structure, integrated intensities of the powder peaks, extract intensities from the powder pattern
For: any

1. start: Fix all and fit only the intensities at the present cell and peak shape; the atoms and the scale factor are not used.
   Actions: fix_parameters; profile_matching
   Success: chi_sq/n is a number.
   Otherwise: Stop and explain the error.
2. zero: Zero shift alone (never with offset_sycos or offset_sysin).
   Actions: refine_parameters {"pattern": "offset_ttheta"}; profile_matching
   Otherwise: undo_refinement
3. cell: Unit cell parameters with the zero shift.
   Actions: refine_parameters {"pattern": "unit cell"}; profile_matching
   Otherwise: undo_refinement
4. width: Peak width: W, then U and V.
   Actions: refine_parameters {"pattern": "pd_instr_resolution.w"}; profile_matching; refine_parameters {"pattern": "pd_instr_resolution.u, pd_instr_resolution.v"}; profile_matching
   Otherwise: undo_refinement (U, V, W correlate strongly) and keep W only.
5. background: Intensities of the background points (broad free peaks and the background correlate: refine it last).
   Actions: refine_parameters {"pattern": "pd_background"}; profile_matching
   Otherwise: undo_refinement

Final answer: chi_sq/n at the start and at the end, the refined cell, zero shift and widths with their errors; the fitted integrated intensities are in the loop pd_peak of every phase (intensity_plus + intensity_minus). The tool refine returns to the Rietveld refinement.
