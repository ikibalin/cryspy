# Magnetic reflections broader than the nuclear ones: a Lorentzian of half width 1/xi in Q for the
# magnetic and interference intensity of the phase (FullProf "magnetic phase" with its own size).
Recipe magnetic_correlation: Magnetic correlation length (magnetic domain size) of a powder phase
Requests: set magnetic domain size, magnetic correlation length, magnetic peaks are broader than nuclear, short-range magnetic order, anisotropic magnetic size, magnetic correlations shorter along c, refine magnetic domain size
For: powder_1d, magnetic

1. start: Fix all parameters and calculate the start model with the nuclear and magnetic structure already refined; the misfit tells whether the magnetic peaks are too narrow.
   Actions: fix_parameters; calculate; diagnose_fit
   Success: chi_sq/n of the start model is a number.
   Otherwise: Stop and explain the error.
2. isotropic: Add the isotropic magnetic correlation length (start 100 A) to the magnetic phase and refine it with the scale.
   Actions: run_procedure {"name": "add_magnetic_correlation_length", "length": 100.0, "phase_label": "", "anisotropic": false}; refine_parameters {"pattern": "scale"}; refine
   Success: chi_sq/n got better and the length is finite and positive.
   Otherwise: undo_refinement: the magnetic peaks are not broader than the nuclear ones (resolution limited).
3. report: The correlation length, its error and the Scherrer size 2 xi.
   Actions: run_procedure {"name": "report_magnetic_correlation"}
4. anisotropic: Only when the isotropic length helped and the misfit of the magnetic peaks depends on hkl: replace it by the ellipsoid started from the refined length (give it as length).
   Actions: run_procedure {"name": "add_magnetic_correlation_length", "length": 100.0, "phase_label": "", "anisotropic": true}; refine; run_procedure {"name": "report_magnetic_correlation"}
   Success: chi_sq/n got better by more than 5 % and the ellipsoid is positive definite.
   Otherwise: undo_refinement and keep the isotropic length.
5. check: What is still wrong.
   Actions: use_recipe {"name": "check_result"}

Final answer: the correlation length xi with its error (or the lengths along a*, b*, c* and the principal lengths of the ellipsoid), the Scherrer size 2 xi and chi_sq/n before and after.
