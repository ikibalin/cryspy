# Brick: the atomic part of a structure, used by the refinement recipes after the scale,
# the background, the cell and the profile are right (a wrong profile spoils the positions).
Recipe refine_atoms: Atomic positions and displacement parameters
Requests: refine the atoms, refine positions and thermal parameters
For: any

1. positions: Free atomic positions; coordinates fixed by the site symmetry stay fixed by themselves (never set them by hand).
   Actions: refine_parameters {"pattern": "atomic positions"}; refine
2. adp: Isotropic displacement parameters of all atoms.
   Actions: refine_parameters {"pattern": "b_iso, u_iso"}; refine
   Otherwise: When a value is negative (often a light atom next to heavy ones): undo_refinement, then refine only the B_iso/U_iso of the heavy atoms (give their paths from list_parameters) and keep the light ones fixed at about 0.5 Å² (B) or 0.006 Å² (U).
3. occupancy: Occupancies, only for mixed or partly occupied sites the user names; never together with the displacement parameter of the same atom, never for fully occupied sites.
   When: the user asks for occupancies or a site is mixed
   Actions: refine_parameters {"pattern": "occupancy"}; refine

Final answer: the positions and displacement parameters with sigmas, the steps undone and why.
