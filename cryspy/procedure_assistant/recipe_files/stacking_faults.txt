Requests: some peaks are broad and others sharp, asymmetric peak, asymmetric low angle peak, peak with a tail to high angles, stacking faults, stacking disorder, layer disorder, polytypes, diffuse peaks, broad hkl peaks, which peaks are broader, diagnose peak widths, background under the peaks is too high
For: any

   Actions: fix_parameters; profile_matching

   Actions: run_procedure {"name": "diagnose_peak_widths"}

3. add: Add the stacking faults suggested by STACKING_FAULTS (normal, shift, probability) and refine the probability with the free intensities.
   Actions: run_procedure {"name": "add_stacking_fault", "normal": "0 0 1", "shift": "0 1/2 0", "probability": 0.1, "phase_label": ""}; profile_matching
   Success: chi_sq/n got better.
   Otherwise: undo_refinement: the broad reflections are not explained by these faults.
4. background: The background under the broad lines is now free: refine the background points and the peak widths with the probability.
   Actions: refine_parameters {"pattern": "pd_background.intensity, pd_instr_resolution.w"}; profile_matching
   Otherwise: undo_refinement
5. report: The layers, the fault shift, the probability with its error and the correlation length of the stacking.
   Actions: run_procedure {"name": "report_stacking_fault"}
6. check: What is still wrong.
   Actions: use_recipe {"name": "check_result"}

Final answer: which reflections are broad (the rule) and why (stacking faults: layers, fault shift), the probability with its error, the correlation length of the stacking, chi_sq/n before and after. If the cell has n layers along the normal, say that the normal n (h k l) gives the probability per layer.
