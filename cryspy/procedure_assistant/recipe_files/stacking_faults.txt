# Some powder reflections broad or asymmetric, the others sharp: stacking faults of the layers (Hendricks-Teller model).
# diagnose_peak_widths finds the rule (k odd, h + k odd, h - k not 3n), the layers and the probability.
Recipe stacking_faults: Stacking faults of a powder phase (only some reflections broad or asymmetric)
Requests: some peaks are broad and others sharp, asymmetric peak, asymmetric low angle peak, peak with a tail to high angles, stacking faults, stacking disorder, layer disorder, polytypes, diffuse peaks, broad hkl peaks, which peaks are broader, diagnose peak widths, background under the peaks is too high
For: any

1. start: Fix all parameters and fit the free intensities of the reflections (profile matching), so that the peak widths are not mixed with the structure.
   Actions: fix_parameters; profile_matching
   Success: chi_sq/n is a number.
   Otherwise: Stop and explain the error.
2. diagnose: Which reflections are broader than the instrument; the fault rule, the layers and the start probability.
   Actions: run_procedure {"name": "diagnose_peak_widths"}
   Success: a line SELECTIVE_WIDTH and a line STACKING_FAULTS.
   Otherwise: Explain the diagnosis (ALL_BROAD: size or strain of the phase; WIDTHS: no group is broader) and stop.
3. add: Add the stacking faults suggested by STACKING_FAULTS (normal, shift, probability) and refine the probability with the free intensities.
   Actions: run_procedure {"name": "add_stacking_fault", "normal": "0 0 1", "shift": "0 1/2 0", "probability": 0.1, "phase_label": ""}; profile_matching
   Success: chi_sq/n got better.
   Otherwise: undo_refinement: the broad reflections are not explained by these faults.
4. background: The background under the broad lines is now free: refine the background points and the peak widths with the probability.
   Actions: refine_parameters {"pattern": "pd_background.intensity, pd_instr_resolution.w"}; profile_matching
   Otherwise: undo_refinement
5. correlation: Are the faults independent? Refine the probability of a fault after a fault too.
   Actions: set_attribute {"path": "stacking_fault.probability_repeat", "value": "0.3"}; refine_parameters {"pattern": "stacking_fault.probability_repeat"}; profile_matching
   Success: chi_sq/n got better and probability_repeat differs from probability by more than 3 sigma.
   Otherwise: undo_refinement: the faults are independent.
6. report: The layers, the stacking operation, the fault shift, the probabilities with their errors and the correlation length of the stacking.
   Actions: run_procedure {"name": "report_stacking_fault"}
7. check: What is still wrong.
   Actions: use_recipe {"name": "check_result"}

Final answer: which reflections are broad (the rule) and why (stacking faults: layers, stacking operation, fault shift), the probabilities with their errors (independent faults, or runs of faults when probability_repeat > probability), the correlation length of the stacking, chi_sq/n before and after.
