# Single-crystal integrated intensities. Extinction parameters at 0 have zero gradient,
# so they get start values first; scale and extinction correlate (|r| about 0.9 is normal).
Recipe refine_single_crystal: Refinement of single-crystal intensities
Requests: refine the single crystal data, fit the integrated intensities
For: single_crystal, unpolarized

1. start: Fix all, calculate and read the misfit.
   Actions: fix_parameters; calculate; diagnose_fit
   Success: chi_sq/n and R_F2 are numbers.
   Otherwise: Stop and explain the error.
2. scale: Scale factor alone.
   Actions: refine_parameters {"pattern": "scale"}; refine
   Otherwise: Stop: without the scale nothing else can be refined.
3. extinction: Extinction radius from the start values 10 μm (radius) and 30' (mosaicity), then the mosaicity. A correlation |r| up to 0.95 with the scale is expected; undo only when chi_sq/n or R_F2 gets worse.
   When: diagnose_fit reports EXTINCTION, or strong reflections are weaker than calculated
   Actions: set_parameter {"path": "extinction.radius", "value": 10, "if_zero": true}; set_parameter {"path": "extinction.mosaicity", "value": 30, "if_zero": true}; refine_parameters {"pattern": "extinction.radius"}; refine; refine_parameters {"pattern": "extinction.mosaicity"}; refine
4. lambda_half: Part of the λ/2 contamination of the beam.
   When: the experiment has setup.ratio_lambdaover2
   Actions: refine_parameters {"pattern": "ratio_lambdaover2"}; refine
5. atoms: The atoms.
   Actions: use_recipe {"name": "refine_atoms"}
6. check: What is still wrong.
   Actions: use_recipe {"name": "check_result"}

Final answer: chi_sq/n and R_F2 at the start and at the end, the extinction parameters, the steps done, skipped and undone with the reason, and the worst reflections.
